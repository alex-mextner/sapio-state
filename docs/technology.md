# Technology programmes

Status: candidate research portfolio

The project proposes testing whether scientific, engineering, and institutional capability can provide forms of international value and cooperation that rely less on coercive power.

This is a research thesis, not a claim that technology eliminates geopolitical conflict.

## 1. Advanced AI / AGI

Research areas:
- capability-secured personal agents;
- local and private inference;
- verifiable tool use;
- model evaluation;
- agent governance;
- open models and reproducible datasets where appropriate;
- safety, control, and incident response;
- AI for science and public administration.

Metrics should include not only benchmark capability but also reliability, energy cost, privacy, security, and real-world usefulness.

## 2. Fusion and abundant energy

Potential programme:
- materials;
- magnets;
- plasma control;
- simulation;
- power electronics;
- tritium-cycle research;
- heat extraction;
- manufacturing;
- regulatory tooling.

The project should not claim a deployment timeline without evidence from the specific technical approach.

## 3. E-government / digital public infrastructure

Research:
- portable identity;
- verifiable credentials;
- rules as code;
- auditable budgets;
- public decision records;
- procurement transparency;
- consent and delegation;
- dispute/appeal workflows;
- secure voting/consultation where appropriate;
- interoperable public APIs.

## 4. Local-first personal computing

Reference platforms:
- AGENT hardware: https://github.com/alex-mextner/AGENT

Research:
- durable entities instead of app silos;
- peer-to-peer synchronization;
- capability security;
- personal data stores;
- offline-first operation;
- user-controlled AI context.

## 5. Communications

Research:
- direct local networking;
- delay-tolerant networking;
- mesh transports;
- satellite fallback;
- interoperable encrypted messaging;
- resilient identity/key discovery;
- emergency communication.

## 6. Manufacturing and robotics

Potential role:
- rapid hardware prototyping;
- open production files;
- distributed manufacturing;
- automation for energy and infrastructure;
- repairability;
- supply-chain traceability.

## 7. Health / biotechnology

If pursued:
- privacy-preserving personal health data;
- open diagnostic tooling;
- low-cost sensing;
- reproducible biomedical research;
- strict consent and safety review.

This area requires particularly strong regulatory and ethics boundaries.

## 8. Programme selection

A technology should not become strategic merely because it is fashionable.

Candidate scoring dimensions for public discussion:
- expected public value;
- uniqueness / neglectedness;
- feasibility;
- available expertise;
- capital intensity;
- externalities and risk;
- time to useful milestone;
- interoperability with existing institutions;
- openness/reproducibility;
- ability to attract or train rare specialists.

No automated score should replace a documented decision.
